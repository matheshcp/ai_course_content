Learning goals

  • Map Excel columns/rows/sheets to Python lists, dicts, and lists-of-dicts
  • Clean messy text and numbers with strip/title and None handling
  • Build reusable cleaning functions and comprehensions
  • Answer revenue-by-region from a 12-row messy sales sheet

Lab intro

Junior Analyst Track: Cleaning a Sales Spreadsheet

Scenario: You're a junior analyst. Someone sends you sales_spreadsheet.csv — 12 rows, messy product names, missing quantities, inconsistent regions. Your job: clean it with pure Python and answer: what's our total revenue by region? You will learn: lists, dicts, loops, functions, comprehensions — all with sales-data examples. Time: ~45 minutes. Level: Beginner. Needs: Python 3.8+ only (no pandas needed).
