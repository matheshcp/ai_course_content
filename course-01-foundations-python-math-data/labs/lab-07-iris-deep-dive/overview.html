Lab intro

Statistics for Analysts Track: Group Stats, ANOVA, Confidence Intervals

Scenario: Classic iris.csv (150 rows, 4 numeric features + species). Compute per-species statistics, a one-way ANOVA on petal length, a 95% CI for virginica sepal width, and publish a pair-plot. You will learn: groupby.agg, variance, F-stat intuition, Cohen’s d, confidence intervals, seaborn/matplotlib pair plots. Time: ~40 minutes. Level: Beginner. Needs: pandas + scipy + matplotlib/seaborn. Env: 🟢 Colab only.
